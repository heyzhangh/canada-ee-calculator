什么是 Express EntryWhat is Express Entry

Express Entry 管理三个联邦项目：Federal Skilled Worker Program、Canadian Experience Class、Federal Skilled Trades Program；部分省提名通道亦通过其发出邀请。申请人先在池中建档并获得 CRS 分数；每轮按类别与分数线由高分到低分邀请，仅获邀者可递交完整永久居民申请。无固定邀请分数线；高分不保证获得邀请。来源：Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) criteria。

Express Entry manages three federal programs: the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Trades Program; some provincial or territorial nomination streams also invite through it. Candidates create a profile in the Express Entry pool and receive a Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score; each round invites from the highest scores down by category and cut-off, and only invited candidates can submit a full permanent residence application. There is no fixed passing score, and a high score does not guarantee an invitation. Source: Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) criteria.

1200 分是怎么构成的How the 1,200 points are built

满分 1200 分：核心与配偶合计上限 500 分，技能组合上限 100 分，加分项上限 600 分。核心上限：无配偶 500 分；有配偶时主申请人 460 分、配偶或同居伴侣 40 分，合计上限仍为 500 分。加分项中 Provincial or territorial nomination 单项 600 分，合计上限仍为 600 分。

The Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) maximum is 1,200 points: Core/human capital factors + Spouse or common-law partner factors combined up to 500 points, Skill transferability factors up to 100 points, and Additional points up to 600 points. With a spouse or common-law partner, the principal applicant ceiling is 460 points, plus up to 40 points for Spouse or common-law partner factors. A Provincial or territorial nomination alone is 600 points.

技能组合Skill transferability factors

分为两组，各上限 50 分：学历组（学历与语言、学历与加拿大工作经验），海外组（海外工作经验与语言、海外工作经验与加拿大工作经验）。Certificate of qualification 单独计分，上限 50 分；三项合计上限 100 分。语言条件以四项最低项为准；加拿大工作经验按 1 年与 2 年及以上两档划分。

Two groups, each capped at 50 points: Level of education combined with Official languages proficiency and Canadian work experience; and foreign work experience combined with Official languages proficiency and Canadian work experience. Certificate of qualification is separate, capped at 50 points; all three together are capped at 100 points. Language qualification is determined by the lowest ability. Canadian work experience uses year bands (1 year, and 2 years or more). Foreign work experience alone receives no Core/human capital factors points.

劳动力市场融合因素详解（2026 年 11 月 3 日新规）Labour Market Integration factor in detail (effective November 3, 2026)

构成：加拿大工作经验选项与合格 Job Offer 选项，取较高者，不叠加；上限无配偶 150 分，有配偶 140 分。工作时长分按现行年数档计分；高薪职业分需在同一级别合计满 12 个月（+20/+40/+70）；Job Offer 分 50/70/100，未列入清单为 0 分。高薪判定以职业中位工资与全国总体平均中位工资的比值为准，清单共 186 个职业，分 3 个级别。来源：2026 changes to points for Canadian work experience and job offers in the Express Entry system。

The Labour Market Integration factor is new in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS), effective November 3, 2026. Canadian work experience and a qualifying job offer are not combined; the higher of the two options applies. Work-length points follow current Canadian work experience year bands; high-wage occupation points (+20/+40/+70) require 12 months in the same level; job offer points are 50/70/100, and 0 for occupations not listed. High-wage status is determined by the occupation's median wage, not by personal salary. The maximum is 150 points without a spouse or common-law partner and 140 points with one. The high-wage list contains 186 occupations in 3 levels. Source: 2026 changes to points for Canadian work experience and job offers in the Express Entry system.

算例：单人、软件开发者、加拿大工作经验 2 年、1.5 倍级别、合格 Job Offer 同级别：经验选项 93 分，Offer 选项 70 分，取 93 分（+40）；有配偶、非高薪职业、2 年、1.5 倍 Offer：经验选项 46 分，Offer 选项 70 分，取 70 分（+24）；单人、5 年、2 倍级别：经验选项 150 分，达到上限。

Examples: single, software developer, 2 years in Canada, 1.5 times level, qualifying job offer in the same level — Canadian work experience option 93, qualifying job offer option 70, higher option 93 (+40); with a spouse or common-law partner, non-high-wage occupation, 2 years, 1.5 times offer — Canadian work experience option 46, qualifying job offer option 70, higher option 70 (+24); single, 5 years, twice level — Canadian work experience option 150 at the cap.

2026 年 11 月 3 日起，合格 Job Offer 不再需要 LMIA，LMIA 不再增加 CRS 分数。Offer 须真实有效，官员核查雇主经营、履行能力、申请人资格与履职可能。LMIF 不改变技能组合、配偶、法语、省提名计分。池内档案重算期间，邀请轮次暂停。

From November 3, 2026, a qualifying job offer does not require a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA). Only a genuine and valid qualifying job offer in an eligible high-wage occupation receives job offer points. Officers assess whether the employer is active, can fulfil the offer, and follows employment and recruitment laws, and whether the candidate is qualified and likely to accept and carry out the job. The changes do not alter Skill transferability factors, Spouse or common-law partner factors, French language skills, or Provincial or territorial nomination. Existing profiles are recalculated when the changes take effect; invitation rounds are paused during recalculation.

高薪职业清单：适用人群与 186 个职业High-wage occupations list: who qualifies and the 186 occupations

适用人群分两类。加拿大工作经验选项：TEER 0、1、2 或 3，符合现行要求，其中高薪职业合计满 12 个月；可连续或不连续、全职或等量兼职、跨多个高薪职业累计；同时多份工作每周最多按 30 小时计入。合格 Job Offer 选项：真实有效、全职连续、成为永久居民后至少 1 年、职位属于清单职业。两类取较高者。项目资格与 CRS 分数分别评估：未列入清单的 Job Offer 不计 CRS 分数，但仍可用于满足 Federal Skilled Worker Program 或 Federal Skilled Trades Program 的项目资格。

There are two qualifying groups. First, Canadian work experience option: Express Entry candidates with qualifying Canadian work experience (TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3, meeting existing requirements), including at least 12 months in eligible high-wage occupations. The 12 months may be continuous or not, full-time or an equal amount of part-time work, and combined across more than one eligible high-wage job; if more than one job was held at the same time, hours are counted only up to 30 hours per week. Second, qualifying job offer option: Express Entry candidates with a genuine and valid qualifying job offer in an eligible high-wage occupation, for full-time, continuous work for at least one year after becoming a permanent resident. The higher of the two options applies. Program eligibility and Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) job offer points are assessed separately.

总分明细怎么看How to read the breakdown

总分 = 核心与配偶合计（上限 500 分）+ 技能组合（上限 100 分）+ 加分项（上限 600 分），合计上限 1200 分。明细分项按封顶前数值列出；小计与合计按上限计入。开启劳动力市场融合因素后，核心中的加拿大工作经验行替换为劳动力市场融合因素行。吸顶四项小计之和等于总分。

Total = Core/human capital factors + Spouse or common-law partner factors combined (capped at 500) + Skill transferability factors (capped at 100) + Additional points (capped at 600), then capped at 1,200 points. Breakdown items are shown before caps; subtotals and totals are capped. When the Labour Market Integration factor applies (effective November 3, 2026), the Canadian work experience row in Core/human capital factors is replaced by a Labour Market Integration factor row. In the sticky bar, the Core/human capital factors subtotal is the Labour Market Integration factor-adjusted amount, so the four sticky subtotals sum to the total.

常见问题FAQ

问：海外工作经验单独计多少分？

答：0 分。海外工作经验不直接计入核心分，仅在技能组合中与官方语言能力或加拿大工作经验组合时计分。

Q: Why does foreign work experience alone score 0?

Foreign work experience does not receive Core/human capital factors points. It is counted only in Skill transferability factors when combined with Official languages proficiency or Canadian work experience.

问：配偶或同居伴侣分数低是否影响总分？

答：核心上限为：无配偶 500 分；有配偶时，主申请人 460 分，配偶或同居伴侣 40 分，合计上限仍为 500 分。配偶或同居伴侣三项上限合计 40 分。

Q: Can a spouse or common-law partner with a low score reduce the total?

With a spouse or common-law partner, the principal applicant's Core/human capital factors ceiling is 460 points, plus up to 40 points for Spouse or common-law partner factors.

问：是否有固定邀请分数线？

答：无固定邀请分数线。每轮邀请分数线按类别、邀请人数和池内分布确定。

Q: Is there a fixed passing score?

No. Each round's cut-off depends on category and on the number of invitations issued in that round.

问：合格 Job Offer 是否仍需 LMIA？

答：2026 年 11 月 3 日起，合格 Job Offer 不再需要 Labour Market Impact Assessment（LMIA），LMIA 也不再增加 CRS 分数。Job Offer 须真实有效；只有高薪职业清单上的 Job Offer 才计 CRS 分数。

Q: Does a qualifying job offer still need a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA)?

From November 3, 2026, no. A Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) does not add Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) points. The offer must be genuine and valid, and only offers in eligible high-wage occupations earn Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) job offer points.

问：如何确认职业是否为高薪职业？

答：以 5 位 National Occupational Classification（NOC）2021 代码是否列入 IRCC 高薪职业清单为准。清单共 186 个职业，分 3 个级别；判定依据为职业中位工资与全国总体平均中位工资的比值，不以个人工资为准。

Q: Is my occupation a high-wage occupation?

Check whether the 5-digit National Occupational Classification (NOC) 2021 code is on IRCC's high-wage occupation list (186 occupations in 3 levels). The level is determined by the occupation's median wage relative to the average median wage of the overall Canadian work force, not by personal salary.

问：语言成绩有效期为多久？

答：2 年。成绩在进入 Express Entry 池时和申请时均须有效；过期须重新考试。四项须来自同一次考试。

Q: How long are language results valid?

2 years. Results must still be valid when entering the Express Entry pool and when applying. Expired results require a retest. All four abilities must come from the same sitting.

问：高分是否一定获得邀请？

答：不一定。分数决定在池中的排名；邀请还取决于类别、轮次、名额。

Q: Does a high enough score guarantee an invitation?

No. The score sets the rank in the Express Entry pool. Invitations also depend on category, round of invitations, and the number of invitations issued in that round.
